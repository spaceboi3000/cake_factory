The Physics of DVFS: Why did the battery drain faster?

Throughput scales linearly with clock frequency ($f$), but dynamic CMOS power dissipation scales cubically ($P \propto V^2 \cdot f \approx f^3$). Operating at 5.0 GHz delivers 5× speed, but consumes energy at a 125× greater rate!
